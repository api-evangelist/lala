# Lala

> Lala (meetlala.io) is an AI content platform for educators. It ingests recordings of
> online classes — either hosted natively on Lala's own video-call product or uploaded as a
> file or link — and automatically produces short-form social video (Reels/TikTok-format
> clips), packaged courses, transcriptions and class summaries. The product is positioned at
> teachers, tutors, music instructors, "teacherpreneurs", schools and universities who want a
> social-media presence without doing the editing work themselves. Lala is a 500 Global
> portfolio company and came out of the 500 Georgia program.

Provenance: generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline on 2026-07-19 from the
public Lala website. Lala publishes no /llms.txt of its own (404), no developer
documentation, and no public API.

## Product

- [Lala home](https://meetlala.io/): AI curation of class recordings into Reels and courses
- [Features](https://meetlala.io/#features): AI curation, auto-reframe to Instagram/TikTok aspect ratios, upload by file or link, transcription and summary after each class, native lesson hosting for up to 200 participants
- [Pricing](https://meetlala.io/#pricing): Free ($0/mo), Pro ($30/mo), Enterprise ($200/mo) — tiered by upload minutes, video-call participants, and Reels generated per class
- [FAQ](https://meetlala.io/#faq)
